## The corrected pipeline (every production, in order)
1. **Writers' Room** — director's treatment → script, using the studio's treatment builder + the script skills above. Glam OKs the treatment before anything builds.
2. **Casting & Graphics** — every still/storyboard frame via the ChatGPT Glam Image Generator plugin; one render shown, Glam approves, then the next. Locked stills are the only faces ever used.
3. **Wardrobe / Music** — Dress My Twin; Song Studio / Glam Radio.
4. **Filming** — images into VideoExpress; the correct built-in workflow for the movie type, playbook settings verified ON in the modal for EVERY generation; Film Crew Farm where the production calls for it.
5. **Director's verdict** — Viv checks the cut against treatment/storyboard/script (identity in every clip, ASR vs script, no pauses >~0.35s, whole-body movement, music bed, 1080×1920/yuv420p). FAIL = defect list + fix, never a dressed-up cut to Glam.
6. **Premiere** — Glam approves the clean cut; only then posting/Glam TV. Nothing posts without her word.

## Standing rules baked into the studio

- One scene at a time for Glam's eye. Glam is visual: results come back as pages/cards/video, not text lists.
- No prices or sold counts in videos, spoken or on screen, ever.
