🎬 THE LOT — SOUND STAGES

Every Glam production walks these 7 steps in order. No step gets skipped — that's how the quality stays up. Check each step off as you finish it.

STAGE 1DIRECTOR'S TREATMENT
1

Writers' Room — Director's Treatment

Every video starts with the full treatment first — concept, script, shot-by-shot, look & feel, music & sound — before anything is built.

Never generate a single frame until the one-page treatment sells the emotion on paper first.

STAGE 2SCRIPT LOCKED
2

Script Office — Script Locked

The script is locked word-for-word before filming. One speaker per shot, every line spoken once — no invented lines on set.

Lock every syllable of dialogue before cameras roll; improvising in the prompt box burns your budget.

STAGE 3CASTING
3

Casting Stage

Cast from her locked character sheets only — every named character's photo goes into the production, even for offscreen voices. Five fingers, one consistent age, frame by frame.

Cast from a locked turnaround sheet so your lead stays the exact same woman in every scene.

STAGE 4DRESS MY TWIN
4

Wardrobe & Makeup

Style the cast in Dress My Twin by Glam — outfits change, faces and bodies never do.

OPEN DRESS MY TWIN →

Wardrobe swaps the silk and sequins, never the silhouette, skin tone, or curves underneath.

STAGE 5SONG STUDIO
5

Scoring Stage — Music

Every production gets a real music bed — made in Song Studio, never silent, never a plain tone.

OPEN SONG STUDIO →

Dead air kills immersion; lay a warm room-tone and music bed under every cut before export.

STAGE 6FILM CREW FARM
6

The Backlot · Filming

The Film Crew Farm does the filming — her 12-agent crew takes the locked script and shoots it scene by scene.

OPEN FILM CREW FARM →

If a shot glitches on the hands or eyes, cut it in the edit suite before audiences ever see.

7

Post-Production & QC — Dex Gate → Premiere

Dex Sterling, Director of Quality, checks the finished cut end to end. Only a clean cut premieres — on Glam TV.
