🎬 The Production Pipeline

Every Glam production walks these 7 steps in order. No step gets skipped — that's how the quality stays up.

1

Director's Treatment

Every video starts with the full treatment first — concept, script, shot-by-shot, look & feel, music & sound — before anything is built.

2

Script Locked

The script is locked word-for-word before filming. One speaker per shot, every line spoken once — no invented lines on set.

3

Casting

Cast from her locked character sheets only — every named character's photo goes into the production, even for offscreen voices. Five fingers, one consistent age, frame by frame.

4

Wardrobe

Style the cast in Dress My Twin by Glam — outfits change, faces and bodies never do.

5

Music

Every production gets a real music bed — made in Song Studio, never silent, never a plain tone.

6

Filming

The Film Crew Farm does the filming — her 12-agent crew takes the locked script and shoots it scene by scene.
